# Seasonal visual-novel backgrounds

Created with built-in `image_gen.imagegen`. Four original spring backgrounds were generated first; each summer, autumn and winter variant was edited from its location's spring reference. Outputs are exact copies of the tool's generated PNGs. No image post-processing was applied.

## Season mapping

| Season | Months | Outdoor cues |
|---|---|---|
| Spring | March–April | Cherry blossoms and new leaves |
| Summer | May–August | Lush green trees |
| Autumn | September–October | Orange, yellow and red foliage |
| Winter | November–February | Bare branches and snow |
