# Property art — editing room, server room, live studio, pop-up market

Created with the built-in `image_gen` tool, one call per final image. No CLI/API fallback, compositing, cropping, resizing, recoloring, or hand-written raster substitutions were used. All 16 deliverables are distinct RGB PNGs at 1672 × 941 pixels (wide 16:9 framing).

## Design and references

- `edit`: compact acoustic editing room with right-side editing desk, three monitors and speakers; open left foreground.
- `server`: clean equipment room with left-side enclosed server racks, cable trays and full-height glass exterior wall; open right aisle.
- `live`: compact broadcast stage with camera, microphones, two empty stools, switcher, lighting rail and curtained corner window.
- `market`: modest pop-up retail shop with wide storefront, modular product shelving, stepped display plinths, round table and cash counter.

The existing `dist/assets/scene-studio-spring.png` and `dist/assets/scene-office-spring.png` were inspected only for visual style. Their architecture, composition and furnishings were not reused. The four spring images were independent new generations with no input-image argument. Each summer/autumn/winter image was generated as a seasonal edit of its own location's spring PNG, using only that spring PNG in `referenced_image_paths`.

Season boundaries: spring March–April, summer May–August, autumn September–October, winter November–February. Spring shows cherry blossoms and fresh leaves, summer lush green foliage, autumn yellow/orange foliage, winter snow and bare deciduous branches.

## Validation

All 16 images were visually inspected. The room architecture and object placement remain consistent within each four-season set; the four locations have distinct layouts. Outdoor seasons are clearly visible, and interiors have no people or animals. Equipment screens and packaging are unlabelled. PNG validity, common dimensions and 16 unique SHA-256 hashes were checked.
